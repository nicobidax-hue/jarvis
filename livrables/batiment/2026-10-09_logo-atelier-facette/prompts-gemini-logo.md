# Prompts Gemini (Nano Banana) : logo Atelier Facette

> À coller sur gemini.google.com (outil "Créer une image"). Prompts en anglais, ils donnent de meilleurs résultats avec les modèles d'image.
> Le nom "Atelier Facette" est provisoire.

---

## Prompt principal

```
Design a minimalist, premium logo for "Atelier Facette", an artisan interior renovation company in France specialising in polished concrete (béton ciré) and resin floors.

Symbol: a simple geometric faceted stone, like a cut gemstone or a mineral seen from the front, built from 4 to 6 clean flat facets. One facet in terracotta, one in soft ochre, the others in warm anthracite. It must stay readable at very small sizes (favicon, Instagram profile picture).

Wordmark: the text "Atelier Facette" written exactly like this, in an elegant modern serif typeface with soft contrast (similar to Fraunces), warm anthracite colour, placed to the right of the symbol. Spell it exactly: A-t-e-l-i-e-r F-a-c-e-t-t-e.

Colours only: warm anthracite #2A2724, terracotta #A65A36, ochre #C29A5B, on a plain warm off-white background #F5F1EA.

Style: flat vector logo, crisp edges, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture, no mockup, no extra text, no slogan. Centered with generous empty space around it. Calm, warm, high-end craftsmanship feeling, not corporate, not tech.

Avoid: houses, roofs, hammers, trowels, paint rollers, brushes, any construction cliché.
```

## Variantes à demander ensuite (dans la même conversation)

**Version empilée (pour Instagram, cartes de visite) :**
```
Same logo, but stacked: the faceted symbol on top, "Atelier Facette" centered below it. Keep exactly the same colours, symbol and typeface.
```

**Symbole seul (pour la photo de profil et l'icône du site) :**
```
Same faceted symbol only, without any text, centered on the off-white background #F5F1EA. Keep exactly the same shapes and colours.
```

**Version claire sur fond sombre (pour le pied de page du site, un véhicule) :**
```
Same horizontal logo on a plain warm anthracite background #2A2724. Text and dark facets become off-white #F5F1EA, terracotta and ochre facets stay the same.
```

**Si le résultat est trop chargé :**
```
Simpler. Fewer facets, thinner and cleaner shapes, more empty space. It should look like a logo drawn by a high-end design studio.
```

**Correction après la première proposition (symbole irrégulier, effet "pierre fissurée") :**
```
I like the colours, the typeface and the overall direction. Keep the wordmark exactly as it is. Redraw only the symbol: make it perfectly regular and symmetrical, like a precisely cut gemstone seen from the front. Use only 5 facets maximum, all edges straight and aligned, all separation lines with exactly the same thin width. It must look precise and crafted, never broken, cracked or like a shattered tile. One terracotta facet, one ochre facet, the rest warm anthracite. Show the horizontal version on the off-white background, and next to it the symbol alone at a very small size to check it stays readable.
```

**Exploration "joyau" (3 pistes de taille de pierre) :**
```
Generate a new image. Let's explore the symbol further. Keep the wordmark "Atelier Facette", the typeface and the colours exactly as they are. Turn the symbol into a jewel: a precisely cut gemstone, flat vector, no gradients, no shine, no sparkles.

Show 3 different options side by side, each one with the symbol placed above "Atelier Facette" (stacked version), on the off-white background #F5F1EA:

1. Emerald cut seen from above: an elongated octagon with concentric step facets, like layers. Clean, architectural, calm.
2. Classic brilliant cut seen from the side: a flat table on top, a short crown and a pointed pavilion below, 5 to 7 facets maximum.
3. Hexagonal cut seen from above: a regular hexagon with a smaller hexagon table in the centre and facets around it.

For every option: perfectly symmetrical, straight aligned edges, all separation lines in the same thin off-white width, one or two terracotta #A65A36 facets, one or two ochre #C29A5B facets, the rest warm anthracite #2A2724. It must look like high-end craftsmanship for an interior renovation company, not like a jewellery store or a diamond shop. Below each option, show the symbol alone at a very small size (32 pixels) to check readability.
```

**Affinage des pistes 1 (émeraude) et 3 (hexagone) :**
```
Generate a new image. Keep only options 1 (emerald cut) and 3 (hexagonal cut), side by side. Keep the wordmark "Atelier Facette", the typeface and the colours exactly as they are, stacked under each symbol.

Refine option 1, emerald cut:
- The outer anthracite frame is too thick and heavy: make it thin, the same width as the other step facets
- Only 2 rings of step facets around the central table, not 3, so it stays readable at small sizes
- Balance the colours symmetrically: central table in terracotta #A65A36, the four small corner facets in ochre #C29A5B, the rest in warm anthracite #2A2724

Refine option 3, hexagonal cut:
- Make it look like a cut gemstone, not like a hex nut: the central hexagon table is slightly smaller and the six surrounding facets are clearly bevelled
- Balance the colours symmetrically: central table in terracotta #A65A36, two opposite facets (top right and bottom left) in ochre #C29A5B, the other four in warm anthracite #2A2724

For both: perfectly symmetrical, sharp straight edges, all separation lines in the same thin off-white width, flat vector, no gradients, no shine, off-white background #F5F1EA.

Under each logo, show the symbol alone inside a small round profile picture (like an Instagram avatar, 64 pixels), on the off-white background.
```

---

## À vérifier sur chaque proposition

- Le nom est bien écrit (les modèles d'image se trompent parfois de lettre)
- Le symbole reste lisible en tout petit : regarde l'image en la réduisant à la taille d'une icône
- Pas de filigrane visible dans un coin de l'image

## Ensuite

Gemini livre une image (PNG), pas un fichier vectoriel. Pour l'impression (cartes, véhicule, panneau de chantier), il faudra la redessiner en vectoriel (SVG), dans Canva ou avec un outil de vectorisation. Je peux t'aider pour cette étape.
